# Halcyon R10

The R10 is built for the circuit alone: the lightest of the range, with the most downforce and the highest revs.

{{specs}}

## Design

A roof scoop feeds the engine, with a fin and a large fixed wing behind it. The car sits lower, the wheels carry visible negative camber, and it runs on slicks.

## Powertrain

A 5.2 l 90° V10 with ten intake trumpets makes 640 N·m at 8000 rpm and 625 kW, with the red line at 10,500 rpm. The 6-speed sequential gearbox has straight-cut gears and shifts in about 0.035 s.

## Chassis and aero

Only 1180 kg with driver, a centre of gravity 0.33 m high, and springs nearly twice as stiff as the H9's. Its downforce lets it corner at 3.4 g in fast bends and brake at over 3 g.

## How it drives

- 325 km/h top speed, 0–100 km/h in 2.2 s: the downforce and the drag that comes with it buy cornering speed, not top speed.
- 3.4 g in the corner program.
- About 3.5 g in the brake program.

## Sound

A 90° V10 on shared crank pins: the banks fire alternately 90° and 54° apart, which makes a sharp howl at high revs, with the whine of the straight-cut gears over it.
