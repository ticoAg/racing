# Halcyon H9

The H9 is the reference model of the Halcyon range: a mid-engine V12 hypercar. The other three models start from its specification and state only what differs.

{{specs}}

## Design

The engine sits behind the cabin. The nose is short and low, and a deep intake in each flank feeds the engine bay. Two radiators sit in the nose; the tail has a grille panel and twin centre exhausts. It can wear a swan-neck GT wing, an active spoiler or no wing; the track kit adds canards to the nose.

## Powertrain

A 6.5 l V12 with a 65° bank angle makes 1045 N·m at 7000 rpm and about 890 kW, with the red line at 9000 rpm. The 7-speed dual-clutch gearbox and differential form one unit behind the engine. In the parts store you can see the two cylinder heads, the twin carbon plenums, the six-into-one manifolds, and a silencer and tail pipe for each bank.

## Chassis and aero

1390 kg with driver, a 42/58 weight split, a centre of gravity 0.40 m high, semi-slick tyres and carbon-ceramic brakes. On a straight above 250 km/h the GT wing trims flat to cut drag; under braking it stands up as an airbrake.

## How it drives

- Cruise program: close to 400 km/h in about 30 s with the GT wing, about 22 s with the road kit.
- Corner program: about 1.85 g through fast esses, with almost no roll.
- Brake program: from about 300 km/h at up to about 2.5 g with the airbrake.

## Sound

Twelve evenly spaced firing pulses per cycle: at high revs, a continuous, piercing scream.
