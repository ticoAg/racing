# Halcyon G8

The G8 is the grand tourer: engine behind the front axle, a long bonnet, the cabin close to the rear axle and a short tail. It runs road tyres and soft springs, and of the four its body moves the most.

{{specs}}

## Design

The nose is long, the flanks have no intake like the H9's, and the tail panel is painted in the body colour. One exhaust pipe per bank runs under the floor to the silencers in the tail.

## Powertrain

A 5.0 l 90° V8 with the supercharger in its V makes 880 N·m at 4200 rpm and 566 kW, with the red line at 7200 rpm. A torque tube under the cabin carries the drive to an 8-speed automatic transaxle at the rear axle.

## Chassis

1720 kg with driver and a 48/52 weight split. The springs are soft and the centre of gravity is higher than the H9's, so the squat under power, the dive under braking and the lean in a corner are all easy to see.

## How it drives

- 335 km/h top speed, 0–100 km/h in 3.6 s.
- About 1.4 g in the corner program.

## Sound

On a cross-plane V8 each bank fires at uneven intervals (180°–270°–180°–90°). The pulses in each manifold alternate between strong and weak, which gives the deep burble typical of this kind of engine, with the supercharger's whine rising over it.
