# Models

The Halcyon range has four cars. All are fictional designs made for this project and have no connection with any real manufacturer. They share a carbon tub, a canopy and butterfly doors; everything else differs. Body curves, powertrain, chassis set-up and sound are each generated from the model's own specification.

{{range}}

The figures are what the simulation produces: acceleration and top speed come from the cruise program, cornering from the corner program.

## Which one

- [H9](/en/models/h9/): the reference. A mid-mounted 6.5 l V12 that revs to 9000 rpm, and a body that stays flat.
- [G8](/en/models/g8/): the long-bonnet grand tourer. A supercharged V8 that burbles, on soft springs: it squats, dives and leans.
- [E4](/en/models/e4/): the electric hypercar. Four motors, 1400 kW, all-wheel drive, 0–100 km/h in 1.9 s.
- [R10](/en/models/r10/): the track car. 1180 kg on slicks with a fixed wing, cornering at 3.4 g.

In the configurator the model is chosen at the top left. A link can open one directly, for example `https://racing.ticoag.fun/?car=r10`.
