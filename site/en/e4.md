# Halcyon E4

The E4 is the electric hypercar and the quickest of the range off the line: four motors, all-wheel drive, no exhaust pipes.

{{specs}}

## Design

With no engine to feed, the nose is closed apart from a cooling intake in each corner, and a light bar runs between the headlights. The tail is long and tapers to cut drag.

## Powertrain

The battery lies flat under the floor. Each axle carries two hollow motors with the half shafts running through them, the reduction gears between them and the inverter above. Together the four motors make 1400 kW and 2057 N·m from a standstill, through a single gear. Off the accelerator the motors regenerate and slow the car.

## Chassis

At 1980 kg with driver it is the heaviest of the range, but the battery sits low: the centre of gravity is only 0.34 m high. In free driving the torque split between the axles follows the load on each.

## How it drives

- 0–100 km/h in 1.9 s, 380 km/h top speed.
- About 1.6 g in the corner program.

## Sound

An electric car has no firing order. Its voice is a set of orders of the motor shaft speed, with one character under power and another under regeneration, rising with road speed.
