# Halcyon: procedural supercars in the browser

Halcyon is a 3D car configurator and driving simulation that runs in the browser. Its four original cars are generated entirely in code — there are no model files — and rendered in real time with three.js. It is free, needs no install or account, and works on desktops and phones. The interface is in Chinese; the controls are icons and short labels.

## What you can do

- **Configure**: 10 colours × 7 finishes, 4 wheel designs, rim and caliper colours, 3 wing options, track and road aero kits, 5 liveries and 4 interiors, plus style presets for each model.
- **Take it apart**: one button lays the parked car out on the studio floor, sorted into nine systems. Click any part to pick it up and turn it; the part under the pointer is named.
- **Driving programs**: watch the car launch to top speed, corner at the limit of grip and brake from 300 km/h on a procedurally generated night circuit.
- **Free driving**: drive by hand in daylight over a 4 km square map with a 10 km road course and 21 bends, with keyboard, gamepad or touch controls.
- **Airflow**: a live 3D lattice-Boltzmann wind tunnel on the GPU shows smoke, the wake and surface pressure.
- **Sound**: each engine is synthesised in real time from its crank angle and firing order. No recordings are used.
